Hi. This is Lancern. Nice to meet you.

My real name is Sirui Mu. Currently I'm a junior employee in the tech team of an HFT company located in Shanghai, China. I graduated from Tsinghua University with a master degree in cybersecurity in 2023. My research area included fuzzing and program analysis. Before that, I graduated from Beijing Institute of Technology with a bachelor degree in software engineering.

I love computer science and computer systems. I started coding when I was 12 (Visual Basic is my first programming language if you wonder) and I always enjoy the process of designing and coding new computer systems. My favorite programming languages include C++, Rust and C#. I'm an enthusiasts of programming languages, compiler construction, and program optimization.

I'm a big advocate of open source and I believe open source can have an impact on everyone on the planet and bring them a better life, either directly or indirectly. Currently I'm focusing on these wonderful projects:

- [pocketpy/pocketpy](https://github.com/pocketpy/pocketpy)

Besides, I'm constantly looking for any open source collaboration opportunities!
